It remains a candidate for independent scientific evaluation, not a certified operational system.

The r11 package recorded 1,717 passing internal checks across nine suites. Re-running tests after moving them verifies the packaging and exercised behavior; it does not create independent scientific validation. Public API sample probes are separate evidence, with time-limited applicability.

Open gates include the P.1147 Figure 4 discrepancy, full-chain ITURHFProp comparison, held-out field observations, installation-specific NEC/antenna validation, real-browser/assistive-technology acceptance, GLM end-to-end decoding and deployment CORS. P.1147 is not extended from its LF/MF domain to 160 m.

A model result, climatology, reported WSPR reception, geometric diagnostic and measurement-conditioned experiment are different kinds of evidence. Do not combine their labels into an unsupported probability or claim of antenna superiority.
